## Why this house works for graduation weekend
Great Lakes Naval Base (Recruit Training Command) is about **20 to 27 minutes** away. Families book this house year-round for PIR (Pass-in-Review) weekends because it is close to the base, spacious enough for the whole family, quiet after long ceremony days, and an easy launch point for celebrating in Chicago afterward.

## Planning the weekend
- **Thursday (Family Day) and Friday (Graduation):** give yourself 30 to 40 minutes of drive time to be safe with base traffic and security lines.
- **Base access:** bring the documents RTC requires (ID and your recruit's division details). Confirm the current visitor policy before you go.
- **After the ceremony:** your sailor often gets liberty. The house is a calm place to reunite, and Chicago is under an hour for a celebration dinner.

## Make it comfortable
- Three bedrooms sleep the family; the living room has extra seating and a swing chair.
- Full kitchen for home-cooked meals between long days on base.
- Single-level layout with no stairs, easy for grandparents.
- Laundry on site if you are staying several nights.

## Nearby for the celebration
- **Kenosha, WI** (16 min): lakefront dining and a relaxed downtown.
- **Chicago** (55 min): the big celebration dinner and skyline.
- **Six Flags Great America** (18 min): if you are traveling with younger siblings.

Many past guests came for exactly this and returned. Message the host if you need a late checkout or an early arrival around ceremony timing.
