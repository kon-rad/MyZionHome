## Built for focus
This home was set up with remote work in mind. It pairs fast WiFi with real workspaces and a genuinely quiet, private setting on 6 acres, which is rare this close to Chicago and Milwaukee.

## The workspace
- **Bedroom 1** has a standing desk, a dual-monitor setup, an ergonomic chair, and a Casper mattress for the end of the day.
- A **dedicated workspace in a room with a door**, so calls and deep work stay undisturbed.
- Additional desks in the bedrooms for a second person.

## The rhythm of the day
- Morning walk on the private forest trail before the first call.
- Focused blocks indoors with the door closed.
- Lunch on the deck; coffee from the moka pot.
- Wind down in the hammock swing or by the fire pit.

## Practical notes
- Long-term stays of 28+ days are welcome.
- Central heating for winter focus; window AC in the bedrooms for summer.
- Walmart 3 minutes away for supplies during a longer stay.
