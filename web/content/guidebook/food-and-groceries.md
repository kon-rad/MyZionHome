## Stocking the kitchen
The kitchen is fully equipped: gas stove and oven, dishwasher, refrigerator, drip coffee maker and a moka pot, kettle, toaster, blender, and a solid set of pots, pans, dishes, and basic spices. Many guests happily cook the whole stay.

- **Walmart** — about 3 minutes away, for groceries and anything you forgot.
- Additional grocery stores and shops are 5 to 10 minutes out.

## Coffee at the house
There is a drip coffee maker and a moka pot, plus complimentary coffee and beverages to start. Bring your favorite beans if you are particular.

## Eating out
Shops and restaurants are 5 to 10 minutes away, with more options across the border in **Kenosha, WI** (16 min): lakefront dining, breweries, custard stands, and casual spots. **Lake Geneva** (44 min) is worth the drive for a nicer evening out.

## A note on water
The home is on well water. It is fine for cooking and cleaning; some guests prefer bottled water for drinking. A quick Walmart run covers it.
