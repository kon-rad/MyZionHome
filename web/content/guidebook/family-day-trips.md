## Six Flags Great America — 18 min
The region's big theme park in Gurnee, with Hurricane Harbor water park next door in summer. An easy day out and back; the quiet house is a welcome reset afterward.

## Bristol Renaissance Faire — ~15 min
A beloved summer-weekend faire just over the Wisconsin line in Kenosha. Jousting, artisans, food, and full-on costumed fun. Under 15 minutes from the house in season.

## RecPlex, Pleasant Prairie — ~10 min
One of the largest recreation complexes around, with pools, ice, and courts. Popular with families in town for tournaments and meets.

## Rainy-day and low-key options
- Kenosha's **Dinosaur Discovery Museum** and public museums (free, family-friendly).
- Movie theaters and shopping in Kenosha and Gurnee.
- Board games, books, and streaming back at the house (Apple TV and Roku).

## Good to know for families
- Single-level home, no interior stairs.
- Big private yard to run around.
- Full kitchen for simple meals and snacks between outings.
- Walmart 3 minutes away for supplies.
