# The Area & Drive Times

The house sits in Zion, Illinois, right near the Wisconsin border, midway between Chicago and Milwaukee. Peaceful and private, but close to a lot.

## Drive times from the house
- **North Point Marina** — 10 min
- **Kenosha, WI** — 16 min
- **Illinois Beach State Park** — 17 min
- **Six Flags Great America** (Gurnee) — 18 min
- **Great Lakes Naval Base (RTC)** — 27 min
- **Lake Geneva, WI** — 44 min
- **Milwaukee, WI** — 46 min
- **Chicago, IL** — 55 min

## Everyday essentials
- **Walmart** is about 3 minutes down the road for anything you forget.
- Grocery stores, shops, and restaurants are 5 to 10 minutes away.
- **RecPlex** in Pleasant Prairie and sports complexes in the area are an easy drive.

## Why guests come here
- **Navy boot camp / RTC graduations** at Great Lakes Naval Base (about 20 to 27 minutes). A very common reason guests book, with easy access to the base and to Chicago.
- **Six Flags Great America** family trips (about 18 minutes).
- **Bristol Renaissance Faire** in Kenosha, WI is under 10 to 15 minutes away in season.
- **Beaches and state parks** on Lake Michigan, marinas, and charter fishing out of Kenosha.
- **Day trips** to Lake Geneva, Milwaukee, and Chicago.
- Quiet remote-work retreats and nature getaways.

## Nearby highlights
- Illinois Beach State Park and Lake Michigan shoreline
- North Point Marina (boating, fishing charters)
- Kenosha, WI: lakefront, dining, shopping, museums
- Lake Geneva, WI: lake resort town, ~44 min
- Downtown Chicago and Milwaukee within about an hour
