# Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I check in?**
Self check-in with a smart lock. You will get a guest key code before arrival; enter it on the door lock. Check-in is any time after 3:00 PM.

**What time is checkout?**
Before 11:00 AM. Just pack up, sign out of any streaming accounts, and lock the door. No need to strip beds or launder towels.

**How many people can stay?**
Up to 6 guests. There are 3 bedrooms, 3 beds, and 2 baths.

**Is it good for a Navy graduation at Great Lakes?**
Yes, very much so. The base (RTC) is about 20 to 27 minutes away, and it is one of the most common reasons guests book. Plenty of space for the family and easy access to the base and to Chicago afterward.

**Is there fast WiFi and a place to work?**
Yes. Fast WiFi throughout, plus a dedicated workspace in a room with a door. Bedroom 1 has a standing desk with a dual monitor and a Casper mattress. Great for remote work.

**Is parking easy?**
Yes, free parking on premises. Two cars fit on the left of the driveway, with more room in front of the garage. Note the current roundabout construction at Russell Rd & Kenosha Rd, which includes a gravel stretch that low-clearance cars should take slowly.

**Is the house air conditioned?**
The bedrooms have window AC units. The living room can get warm on the hottest days. Central heating keeps it warm in winter.

**How is the water?**
The home is on well water. Sink pressure is softer than city water and the shower pressure is good. Some guests prefer to bring bottled water for drinking. This is normal for the property.

**Can I cook?**
Yes. Full kitchen with a gas stove and oven, dishwasher, refrigerator, coffee maker and moka pot, kettle, toaster, blender, and a good stock of pots, pans, dishes, and basic spices.

**Is it kid-friendly and family-friendly?**
Yes. It is spacious, single-level with no interior stairs, and has a big private yard. Many families stay here.

**Are pets allowed?** / **Can I smoke?**
Please message the host about pets before booking. The home is non-smoking indoors.

**Is it private / quiet?**
Very. 6 private acres, far from neighbors, corn fields on one side. Songbirds in the morning, deer on the property. No parties allowed.

**Are there cameras?**
Three exterior security cameras only (driveway, front door, rear door). None inside the home.

**Is there laundry?**
Yes, a free washer and dryer in the basement.

**How do the TV and speakers work?**
43-inch HDTV with Apple TV, plus a Roku TV in the kitchen. Cast from your phone or laptop, or sign in with the remote (sign out before checkout). Connect to the "Sonos Speaker" over Bluetooth for music.

**What is nearby for groceries?**
A Walmart is about 3 minutes away; other shops and restaurants are 5 to 10 minutes out.

**Can I stay long term?**
Yes, stays of 28 days or more are allowed.

**How do I reach the host?**
Message Konrad through Airbnb. Response rate is 100%, usually within an hour.
