# Check-in, Access & Logistics

## Check-in / Checkout
- **Check-in:** after 3:00 PM
- **Checkout:** before 11:00 AM
- **Self check-in** with a smart lock. You will receive a guest key code before arrival. Enter the code to unlock the door.
- No need to meet anyone. Konrad is reachable by message and responds within an hour if you need anything.

## Getting here & parking
- There is parking space for two cars on the left-hand side as you pull into the driveway. There is space for more cars in front of the garage door. Free parking on premises.
- **Construction note:** there is roadwork at Russell Rd & Kenosha Rd (a roundabout is being built). The house is accessible, but the route currently includes a gravel section that may be difficult for very low-clearance cars. Drive slowly through that stretch.

## Smart home quick tips
- **Lights:** Say "Alexa, turn on the living room lights" to operate the floor lamps in the living room. Most rooms are lamp-lit and controlled by switches near the exit doors (the home has no ceiling fixtures in some rooms, which is normal for it).
- **Sound:** Connect to the Sonos speaker over Bluetooth from your phone. Look for a device named "Sonos Speaker".
- **TV / casting:** Cast media to the Apple TV, or to the Roku TV in the kitchen, from your phone or laptop. You can also sign in to your own streaming accounts with the remote. Please remember to sign out of your accounts before checkout.
- **Climate:** Ask Alexa for the weather or to control the thermostat in the hallway.
- **Alexa** can also give you local recommendations.

## Laundry
- Free washer and dryer, located in the basement.

## Checkout
- No need to strip the beds or wash towels. Just pack up, sign out of any streaming accounts, lock up, and go.

## House rules
- 6 guests maximum.
- No parties.
- Check-in after 3:00 PM, checkout before 11:00 AM.
- Long-term stays (28+ days) are allowed.

## Safety
- Three exterior security cameras: one facing the driveway, one by the front door, and one by the rear door. There are no cameras inside the home.
- Smoke alarm, carbon monoxide alarm, fire extinguisher, and first aid kit on site.
- There is a nearby lake, river, or other body of water in the wider area.
